About

This site is the public digital home for Enamul Haque, CTO of ServiceNow Practice at Wipro. It brings together selected work across enterprise technology, ServiceNow strategy and architecture, AI transformation, cybersecurity education, books, courses, and community focused learning.

Core Positioning

Enamul works at the intersection of enterprise architecture, ServiceNow platform strategy, delivery governance, AI enabled transformation, and practical education. At Wipro, he leads the ServiceNow Delivery Centre of Excellence and Innovation and the ServiceNow AI Practice Office. His public work focuses on helping professionals, learners, and organisations understand technology with structure, clarity, and business context.

Public Content Boundary: This site is intentionally limited to public safe material. It does not host confidential client information, internal delivery artefacts, restricted documents, private project records, credentials, API keys, or commercially sensitive content.
