About

This site is the public digital home for Enamul Haque. It brings together selected work across Intelligent Solutions, ServiceNow Platform Strategy and Architecture, AI transformation, cybersecurity education, books, courses, teaching, and community focused learning.

Core Positioning

Enamul works at the intersection of enterprise technology, platform strategy, AI-enabled transformation, and practical education. AI Horizons (2024), published by Mercury Learning and Information and distributed by De Gruyter, is his most widely cited recent work. His cloud computing guide is on the recommended reading list for MSc Computer Science students at the University of the West of England. He presented at ServiceNow Knowledge 2023 in Las Vegas, and holds an Adjunct Professor appointment at Bangladesh Maritime University.

Public Content Boundary: This site is intentionally limited to public safe material. It does not host confidential client information, internal delivery artefacts, restricted documents, private project records, credentials, API keys, or commercially sensitive content.
